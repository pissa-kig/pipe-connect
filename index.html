PIPE CONNECT

DOTS: 0/0
SPACE: 0/36

HOW TO PLAY

  • • Connect Dots: Drag from one colored dot to another of the same color.
  • • No Overlaps: Pipes cannot cross each other or pass through foreign dots.
  • • Fill the Grid: To complete a level, you must connect all dot pairs and cover all 36 spaces on the board!
